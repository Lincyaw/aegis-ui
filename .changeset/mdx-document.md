Add `MdxDocument` for long-form documents written in MDX, such as articles and reports, with `mdxTableOfContents` for their headings, the `aegisMdx()` Vite plugin under `@lincyaw/aegis-ui/vite` that compiles them, and the `.mdx` module types under `@lincyaw/aegis-ui/mdx-modules`. `Markdown` block quotes now sit on a tinted block with every corner rounded, in place of a one-sided rule.
