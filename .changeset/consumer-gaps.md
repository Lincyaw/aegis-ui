`Toolbar variant="page"` wraps and shrinks its right slot at 768 px or narrower instead of letting its selectors overflow sideways. `Select` takes `labelPlacement` (`above` by default); `inline` sets the label inside the control before the value, after the locale's new `labelSeparator`, and it still names the select. `Pane` forwards a ref to its scrolling element, and `DetailBeside` `layout="columns"` takes `scrollContainer="nearest"`, the nearest element around the list that scrolls, such as its `Pane`; a new scroll container reveals the selected row anew, and inside an element the detail is no taller than that element's view. `DataTable` takes `density` (`default` | `compact`, rows `--data-table-compact-row-min-height` high with tighter cells) and `header` (`filled` | `plain`, no header fill). `TimeDisplay` takes `mode="utc-day"` with `now`, the host's clock: `HH:MM` in UTC on now's UTC day, else `MM-DD HH:MM`. `Timeline` takes `mode="list"`: a list whose selected item is marked `aria-current`, and whose items with an `href` are links rendered by `linkComponent`. `LinkComponent` passes on `aria-label`, and its `aria-current` may be `'true'`; `IdentityCell` takes `ariaLabel` for its link. `AnchoredPopover` takes `minWidth`, at least `--anchored-popover-min-width` wide, for content sized by its container such as a `MetadataList`. A `bleed` app's shell `main` is positioned, so an `AnchoredPopover` drawn in it is placed in its box.
